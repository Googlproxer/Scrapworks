Asset credits

Scrapworks uses original procedural world geometry and the following art assets.

Protagonist

Model created with Meshy — CC BY 4.0. Exported from Meshy's free version and modified in Blender by the Scrapworks creator. Changes include geometry and outfit edits, UV correction, torso alignment, rigging, material preparation, animation retargeting and a runtime GLB export.

Rigging and animation from Adobe Mixamo. Mixamo usage information. The Meshy model's CC BY 4.0 notice does not relicense Adobe animation content.

The current model is assets/models/protagonist-v3.glb. These credits do not imply endorsement.

Branding and menu artwork

The Scrapworks logo, gear-and-leaf favicon, cursor and restoration landscape menu illustration were generated for this project with OpenAI's image generation tool. Original images, exact prompts and preparation records are retained in the project's art/ source directory.

Nature models

Small rocks, grass and shrub forms come from KayKit Forest Nature Pack 1.0 by Kay Lousberg, under CC0. Scrapworks adjusts pivots and axes, groups blades and foliage, and uses a shared painted palette. Original sources and the supplied licence remain under art/world/.

Procedural presentation

Machine, transport and connection geometry, terrain patterns and scatter placement are original Scrapworks TypeScript source, distributed under the repository's MIT licence.

Painted surface lighting adapts Babylon.js CellMaterial by the Babylon.js contributors, under the Apache 2.0 licence. Scrapworks adds narrow blends between the original lighting levels and scene-owned resource cleanup. Editable shader source, attribution and modification notes are retained under art/shaders/.

Third-party software notices are distributed separately in 3rdpartylicenses.txt.
