## Local health

Health starts at zero on every cell and accumulates within 0–100%. Apply environment work once per 20 simulation ticks. Accumulate actual supplied greenhouse/scrubber work and actual coal emissions between updates and save those budgets. Use Manhattan distance rings, extending across the full map as nearer cells saturate; there is no fixed greenhouse-radius limit. At each ring, contributors equally share each cell's unmet health, bounded by their remaining budgets, and unused shares are redistributed. Negative coal influence is local within three cells, shared across that footprint; subtract net signed influence before clamping so positive influence can fully offset it. Do not duplicate capped contributions as frontier overflow. Removal alone does not lower health. Reaching 100% across all 400 cells latches the checkpoint and its simulation tick; subsequent pollution does not revoke completion.

## Water conservation and quality

Cardinally connected lake cells form a stable source identified by their lowest cell index. Each supplies 1,000 L initial capacity. Source withdrawal is uniform over all cells, and pumped quality is their mean health. Outside rain, each lake cell refills 0.01 L/s; during rain it refills 1 L/s. Refill has the receiving source's current quality and does not itself restore health. Outstanding water reserves source capacity: natural refill can replace consumed water, but cannot occupy the space needed to return pipes/tanks' extracted-but-unconsumed water. Thus `volume + outstanding <= capacity`, and source accounting always satisfies `outstanding = extracted - consumed - returned = attributed network litres`.

Water batches retain source, litres and quality. Each buffer keeps at most two pools per source: clean bypass water and untreated water with volume-weighted mean quality. Splitting and consumption proportionally divide source pools; merging never erases source attribution. Every positive volume is retained, including microscopic split fractions; only zero-volume records are removed. Fully clean quality is normalized to exactly 100 to avoid floating-point-only treatment costs. Treatment converts up to 4 L/s to quality 100, scaling cost/work with the untreated fraction. Already clean water passes through without treatment work or power cost, still obeying throughput and capacity. Greenhouses require quality-100 water; coal accepts any quality, with emissions multiplied from 1× for clean to 2× for untreated water. Coal uses 0.0002 L/J. Returning treated water to its source grants up to four cell-health points per returned litre, scaled by quality improvement over the source, and never duplicates volume or rewards moving unchanged water.

A normal shoreline outfall targets its adjacent lake. Cross-lake returns require unreserved receiving capacity, improve that receiving source's quality and record paired transfer-in/out totals; the originating source's outstanding attribution is settled exactly. Dismantling instead returns every remaining batch in the affected connected water network to its extraction source, including disabled branches. Committed partial coal fuel energy and battery charge dissipate on dismantling; intact buffered coal and embodied solids recover once, and already burned fuel/water never receive a refund.

Pipes have explicit directed adjacent cardinal ports. Treatment has separate raw input and clean output; water never uses item belts. A contiguous pipe run equalizes its stored volume across segments without pressure. Junctions divide available service fairly among ordinary routes with reachable space; a nominated world cardinal overflow route is used only after ordinary routes have no reachable capacity. Connections are limited by 8 L/s; a phase-start batch moves through at most one external edge per tick, without catch-up credit. Pump demand disappears when no capacity remains. Dismantling returns all affected unconsumed batches to their attributed sources atomically with solid recovery; disconnecting retains owned water. The numerical implementation must validate lake/outstanding conservation, all capacity/rate limits and topology before loading a candidate world.

## Weather and energy

Keep the four-minute daylight cycle and ordinary 250–500 W wind. Seeded weather schedules the next event 180–300 active seconds away, for 45–75 seconds, with rain selected about two thirds of the time. Announce events 30 seconds ahead. Rain multiplies solar by 0.4 and wind by 1.5; Dunkelflaute multiplies solar by 0.2 and wind by 0.05, overriding the normal floor. Save the schedule, durations, PRNG and current event; no wall-clock/offline advancement. Forecast only reveals weather within its announcement window, using known curves otherwise.

Coal ramps up over 12 s and down over 8 s, with a 10 s minimum stable hold. Forecast the next 60 s using known weather and current runnable demand. Stop only if forecast renewables can cover grid demand and required battery charging; restart when that forecast is insufficient. Coal is base load, can charge batteries and takes dispatch precedence during transitions; curtail renewables rather than bypassing the ramp. Batteries discharge into remaining deficits and retain proportional runnable consumer sharing. Charge from real surplus at at most 1.2 kW, crediting 95%; never charge and discharge a battery in the same step. Reconcile against actual productive work, including partial final operations and shared lab work. Coal fuel and water are spent only for actual generated joules, with partial fuel saved. Base emissions are 0.0006 local health points/J, doubled at quality zero; mitigation research multiplies emissions by 0.6 and unlocks the powered scrubber.
