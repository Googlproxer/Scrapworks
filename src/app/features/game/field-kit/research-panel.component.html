Laboratories and later branches

Every technology supports Field Research. Powered supplied laboratories work at {{ labRate }}× the field rate each when locally supplied and powered, all on the same queue head. Laboratories can be crafted and placed from the Factory panel after their research.

Basic fabrication accepts exactly one ingredient type, Medium exactly two, and future Advanced three or four. Safe research kits remain handcraftable and MiniFab-compatible. Personal scrap recovery and mixed-output recycling have separate rules.
