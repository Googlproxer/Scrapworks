# Scrapworks water infrastructure concept sheets v1

Bundled 24 November 2026.

This pack contains the current approved or selected five-stage upgrade concept sheets for the Scrapworks water infrastructure machines.

Included sheets:
- water-pump-upgrade-overview.png
- water-treatment-upgrade-overview.png
- water-tank-upgrade-overview.png
- water-return-upgrade-overview.png

Notes:
- Water Pump: approved working reference. Intake pipe drops into the water edge and the outlet connection is side-mounted.
- Water Treatment: approved for now. Uses a two-stage treatment read with an open settling basin and a distinct secondary treatment vessel.
- Water Tank: the included sheet is the chosen visual reference, even though the displayed inlet/outlet pipes turn downward incorrectly. For modelling and cleanup, keep the approved visuals but correct both ports so they face outward horizontally near ground level.
- Water Return: approved working reference. Square outlet retained, with the Stage 1 valve wheel reduced in size.
- These are concept-overview sheets only, not model sheets or final technical drawings.
