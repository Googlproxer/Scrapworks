## Modelling constraints

Preserve the square top feed hopper, compact crusher/processing body, side drive housings, low front discharge chute, separate power connection and stationary 2 m × 2 m logical footprint from the [M10 recycler concept](../m10-2026-11-12/assets/recycler.png). Keep ports and orientation consistent across upgrades. Reconcile any differences between generated views and authoritative gameplay contracts before modelling. The sheets are illustrative references, not dimensionally authoritative orthographic drawings. Gauges, piping and status lights are visual suggestions, not new gameplay mechanics. Retain a heavy industrial silhouette, subtle early-stage steampunk influence, broad flat-painted colour planes and cream/navy/coral palette. Use the [actual title logo](../../branding/title-logo-v1.png) for final branded presentations; generated logos are not authoritative.
