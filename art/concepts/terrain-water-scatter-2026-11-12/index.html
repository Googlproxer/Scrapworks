To reduce AI generation, start with KayKit's free nature tier. The other packs provide alternative shapes and useful published geometry budgets. These are potential sources; no models have been downloaded or imported. Triangle counts are creator-published.

KayKit Forest Nature Pack

Kay Lousberg · Free tier: 100+ models · Creator lists CC0

Grass, bushes and small rock variations

glTF, FBX, OBJ. Per-model counts not published on the reviewed page.

Best broad starting candidate. Shared palette/gradient atlas; includes an unshaded grass version. Check individual triangle counts and blade geometry before dense scattering.

Low-poly Forest Kit

Toolbench · Free · Creator lists CC0 1.0

Measured-budget grass, rocks and bushes

GLB, FBX, OBJ. Published LOD0/LOD1: grass 90/10, small rock 80/20, bush 320/60 triangles.

Useful budget comparison. FBX contains both LODs; GLB is LOD0. Grass uses paired thin surfaces, and several material slots may need consolidation.

Ultimate Nature Pack

Quaternius · Free / optional donation · Creator lists CC0

Large library of faceted nature shapes and rock/plant variants

Blend, FBX, OBJ. Per-model counts not published on the reviewed page.

150 models. Editable Blender files help recolouring/simplification; export selected models to GLB. Inspect actual small-prop counts and materials.

Quiet Grove Low-Poly Nature Kit

polyhangar · Free / pay what you want · Creator lists CC0 1.0

Small rock and shrub supplement

GLB, OBJ/MTL. Published: basalt rock 80, rock cluster 240, evergreen bush 240 triangles.

Eight flat-colour assets with ground-centred pivots. No grass, UVs or LODs listed; the evergreen bush and basalt rock are the relevant pieces.
