## Modelling notes

Keep the two-legged support frame, cylindrical upper process housing, central fabrication press, front work platform and side power/control box. Stage 2 retains some Stage 1 gear/gauge detail not shown in the concept; Stage 3 and 4 have residual gear/pressure-gauge callout labels that should not be interpreted as additional components. Preserve the logical footprint, port locations and orientation across all stages. Generated views, dimensions, annotations and branding are illustrative references, not authoritative engineering or gameplay specifications.
