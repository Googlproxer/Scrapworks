# Scrapworks logistics concepts v1

Selected visual direction: 24 November 2026.

## Conveyor

[Five-stage conveyor concept](conveyor-upgrade-overview.png)

- Five upgrades communicate increasing transport speed and item throughput.
- Use a slim, repeatable belt section with minimal ground contact, inspired by Satisfactory's modular conveyors. Supports should be sparse and separate from repeating belt geometry.
- Maintain consistent belt width, connection height and connection positions across stages. Adjacent sections should form a visually continuous conveyor without conspicuous repeated end housings or gaps.
- Progress from weathered salvage construction to clean, advanced engineering using the established flat-painted finish and cream/navy/coral palette, with restrained cyan accents at the advanced end.
- Items shown on the belts illustrate throughput; they do not specify gameplay rates or capacity.
