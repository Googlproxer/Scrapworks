Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: usable 2D terrain tileset PNG atlas for Tiled, for the game Scrapworks.

Composition: exact 4 columns by 4 rows, 16 equal rectangular cells, no margins and no spacing, every cell completely filled by its terrain texture. Keep content legible when reduced to that size. Horizontal row boundaries at one quarter, one half and three quarters of the canvas; vertical column boundaries at the same fractions.
Row 1 (top): four subtle variants of dusty warm brown DIRT, with broad simple earth patches and just a few small flat pebble shapes.
Row 2: four subtle variants of cool neutral grey STONE ground / exposed bedrock, with broad flush rock plates, sparse restrained cracks, no raised boulders and no wall blocks.
Row 3: four subtle variants of calm muted teal-blue WATER, with sparse compact flat pale ripple shapes, no shore, no foam border, no land mixed in, no reflections of objects.
Row 4 (bottom): four subtle variants of muted natural green GRASS, with broad grouped patches and a few simple small grass strokes, completely grass covered, no bare dirt borders.
Style: hand-painted 2D game terrain, clean flat colour areas, a base and a shadow tone with very restrained highlights, small coherent natural palette, simple shapes, quiet low contrast within a material. Match the broad flat-shaded finish of a painted 2D character. No pixel art and no photographic texture or grain. Each row keeps the same average colour and same scale of surface detail across all four variants.
View: terrain ground plane seen from an elevated orthographic camera about 55 degrees above the ground, looking along one grid axis, north at the top. Rectangular axis-aligned ground footprints, no isometric diamonds, no visible tile side faces, no beveled tile rims, no cast tile shadows.

Keep all four outer edges of every cell at a consistent material base colour; keep small decorative details inside, away from cell boundaries. No edge darkening, no vignette, no frame and no visible divider lines. Flat lighting and consistent colour right to cell boundaries. Only abrupt material changes between rows; do not draw lines between the columns.
Avoid: text, labels, numbers, logos, watermark, checkerboard, gutters, outlines around tiles, gaps, transparency, buildings, characters, trees, flowers, equipment, landscape composition, gradients or realistic rendering. Deliver only the clean tile atlas artwork.
